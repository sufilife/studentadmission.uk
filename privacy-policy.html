Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At STUDENT ADMISSION LONDON (“we”, “our”, or “us”), accessible via https://studentadmission.uk, we are committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you use our website or services.

By accessing our website or submitting your information, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information
  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number / WhatsApp
  • Nationality
  • Academic background
  • English test results (IELTS / Duolingo / TOEFL / MOI)
  • Study preferences and funding information
  • Any documents you voluntarily submit
Automatically Collected Information
  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages visited and interaction data

These are collected through cookies and analytics tools to improve our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide free counselling and study abroad guidance
  • Assess scholarship eligibility
  • Match you with suitable UK universities and courses
  • Contact you regarding applications, visas, or services
  • Send eligibility results to students and counselors
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Maintain internal records and compliance

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Scholarship Eligibility System

When you use our Scholarship Eligibility Checker, we may store:

  • Your submitted answers
  • Eligibility score
  • University matches
  • Counselor notes (if applicable)

This data is used for follow-up support, offer conversion tracking, historical analysis, and improving recommendation accuracy.

4. Email Communication

By submitting forms on our website, you consent to receive emails from STUDENT ADMISSION LONDON related to eligibility results, application guidance, visa support, and service updates.

Emails are sent via our secure SMTP mail server. You may request removal at any time by emailing admin@studentadmission.uk.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Encrypted database connections
  • Secure servers
  • Restricted admin access
  • Role-based authentication (Admin / Counselor)

We use MongoDB Atlas for data storage and take reasonable technical measures to protect your data. However, no online system is 100% secure.

6. Sharing of Information

Your data may be shared only with:

  • Internal counselors
  • Partner universities (when required for applications)
  • UK authorities (if legally required)

We do not disclose your information for third-party marketing.

7. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to remember preferences, analyze traffic, and improve performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

8. Your Rights

  • Request access to your data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Withdraw consent
  • Ask how your data is used

To exercise these rights, email us at: admin@studentadmission.uk

9. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for students aged 16+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
